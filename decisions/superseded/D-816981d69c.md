- status: active

- route_keywords: route_check_explicit_reopen, ALLOWED_WITH_REOPEN

## 结论
v0.45 keeps rejected route checks blocked by default, but allows a matching rejected decision to pass only when the caller explicitly supplies --reopen-decision and --reopen-reason and the decision has reopen_condition. It records reopened_decision_ids_json and reopen_reason in route_checks. Verified by full unittest suite.
